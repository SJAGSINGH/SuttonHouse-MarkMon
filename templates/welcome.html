SYSTEM BRIEFING

Sutton House is a structured market monitoring system built to reduce unnecessary decision-making.

It defines macro conditions, tracks cycle progression, and interprets market behaviour to determine when participation is permitted.

The system is designed to govern behaviour rather than encourage activity. It does not exist to produce constant signals. It exists to define conditions, enforce discipline, and preserve decision quality.

Signals are observed, not followed.
Silence is a valid state.
